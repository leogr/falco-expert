## Approved jobs and chains

For each active job: approved scope and deliverable, decision that authorized it, current result, remaining work, owner and stopping point. Record any public actions explicitly included; unknown later public steps remain unapproved. Link specialist artifacts and relevant source revisions.

## Public actions

Append one concise entry around each attempted action: approval reference, exact target/content artifact, attempted time, returned object, verification result, and unresolved effects. Distinguish prepared, attempted, observed and uncertain. Never replay an uncertain action on resume.

## Knowledge to retain

Link reusable project findings and specialist reports with their source revisions, verified conclusions, unresolved hypotheses and relevant test results. Preserve enough context to reuse the work after compaction; refresh facts that affect the next decision. Keep private security details out of these notes.

## Process learning

Keep brief entries only when an observation could improve future work. A useful entry records:

- Approach and coverage: what was explored, alternatives considered, why this work was selected; effort/cost if known.
- Expected benefit, actual outcome and maintainer feedback, with evidence links. Leave unknown outcomes explicit.
- Tentative lesson: applicable context, uncertainty, contrary evidence and current status.
- Next trial or proposed improvement, and what result would help assess it; any human decision still needed.

Carry relevant lessons forward with links to their original observations. Use them within approved scope; durable skill changes need review. Approval is not evidence of benefit, and silence is not feedback.

## Changes and decisions

Append dated meaningful deltas, changed rankings, corrections, approvals/rejections and their reasons. Link evidence rather than copying it repeatedly. Keep the newest resume summary accurate. Private security details do not belong here.
